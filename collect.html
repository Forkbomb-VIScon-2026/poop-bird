Before we start

What we record. While you follow on-screen prompts (relax, strain, puff your cheeks), your browser measures your facial expression: 52 expression scores and 478 face-mesh points per video frame, plus your device type, browser and camera name. We never record video or images.

Why. To tune how Poop Bird detects straining and puffing on different faces, in different light and on different devices.

Who sees it. Only the Poop Bird team. It is stored on our team server for the VIScon 2026 hackathon and copied to team members' laptops for analysis.

How long. Until the team server is deleted after the project. Copies on team laptops are deleted at the same time.

Deleting it. You get a participant code, no name. You can delete a session right after recording it, or send us your code and we delete everything recorded under it.
